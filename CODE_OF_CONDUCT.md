# Code of conduct

Unclean contributors must keep project spaces safe, focused, and useful.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss the work, evidence, and tradeoffs without attacking the person.
- Give clear technical feedback and accept corrections when evidence changes.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and license boundaries.
- Make room for contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Stop conduct that threatens, harasses, humiliates, stalks, discriminates, or sexualizes.
- Do not publish private messages, personal information, credentials, customer data, or security
  details without permission.

These rules apply in repository discussions, reviews, project chat, events, and public
representation of the project.

## Reporting conduct

Report conduct concerns through the repository's private reporting channel. If no private
channel exists, ask a maintainer for a private contact without naming the people involved in
public.

Maintainers will review available evidence, limit disclosure to people who need it, and choose a
response based on impact and recurrence. Responses may include guidance, a warning, content
removal, temporary restrictions, or a permanent ban.

Maintainers must step aside from a report when they are involved in the conduct or cannot review
it impartially.
